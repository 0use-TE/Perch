## Dinosaur game pictures (`src/Perch/Assets/Dino`)

The sprites (dinosaur, cacti, bird, cloud, track, "game over" and restart icon) are the pictures of Chrome's offline
dinosaur game, created for the Chromium project by Sebastien Gabriel. Chromium is released under the BSD 3-Clause license,
Copyright 2015 The Chromium Authors. The files used here were taken as separate frames from the repository
https://github.com/dhhruv/Chrome-Dino-Runner (a pygame copy of the game), cropped to their outlines, and their colours
inverted to light grey on black (Chrome's dark mode look). `Services/Games/DinoMasks.cs` (collision shapes) and the one-glyph icon font `Assets/Fonts/PerchIcons.ttf` (the dinosaur on the navigation bar) are generated from them.
